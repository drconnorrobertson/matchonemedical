About Match One Medical

Pioneering drug-free pain management to help physicians and patients break free from the opioid cycle.

Our Story

Built to Fight the Opioid Crisis

Match One Medical was founded by Chaz Jannuzi after witnessing firsthand the devastating toll opioid addiction takes on patients, families, and communities. Recognizing that physicians needed better tools to manage pain without resorting to narcotic prescriptions, Chaz set out to build a company that would put proven, drug-free technologies directly into the hands of healthcare providers.

What began as a focused effort to bring thermal therapy devices to orthopedic practices has grown into a comprehensive medical device and durable medical equipment company serving pain management specialists, orthopedic surgeons, primary care physicians, and sports medicine providers across the country.

Today, Match One Medical offers a full suite of FDA-cleared pain management devices—including the Therm-X, VascuTherm, and Micro-Z product lines—alongside turnkey ancillary revenue programs that make it easy for practices to integrate drug-free treatment modalities into their standard of care.

Leadership

Meet Our Founder

Chaz Jannuzi

Founder & CEO

Chaz Jannuzi founded Match One Medical with a clear mission: give physicians the tools they need to treat pain without contributing to the opioid epidemic. With deep experience in medical device distribution and healthcare operations, Chaz recognized that the gap between available drug-free technology and physician adoption was costing lives.

Under his leadership, Match One Medical has grown into a trusted partner for practices nationwide, providing not only cutting-edge DME products but also the billing infrastructure, compliance support, and patient education resources that physicians need to make drug-free pain management a standard part of their care delivery.

Chaz continues to lead the company's expansion into new treatment modalities, including concussion therapy and advanced spinal orthotics, while maintaining an unwavering focus on the mission that started it all: reducing opioid dependency through innovation.

Our Journey

Company Milestones

Company Founded

Chaz Jannuzi launches Match One Medical with a focus on thermal therapy devices for orthopedic post-operative care.

Opioid Reduction Program Launched

Match One introduces its multi-modality opioid reduction program, combining thermal therapy, compression, and microcurrent stimulation into a single protocol.

VascuTherm Product Line Expansion

The VascuTherm 5 and VascuTherm 2 are added to the product portfolio, offering combined heating, cooling, and pneumatic compression in a single device.

Concussion Therapy Program

Match One expands into post-concussion syndrome treatment using Micro-Z microcurrent neurostimulation technology.

National Physician Network

Over 50 physician practices across the United States join the Match One Medical partner network, integrating drug-free pain management into their standard of care.
