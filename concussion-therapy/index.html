Post-Concussion Syndrome

When Concussion Symptoms Do Not Resolve

A concussion is a mild traumatic brain injury caused by a blow or jolt to the head that disrupts normal brain function. While most concussions resolve within two to four weeks, a significant percentage of patients develop post-concussion syndrome (PCS)—a condition where symptoms persist for weeks, months, or even years after the initial injury.

Common PCS symptoms include persistent headaches, difficulty concentrating, memory problems, dizziness, fatigue, irritability, sleep disturbances, and sensitivity to light and noise. Traditional treatment has relied heavily on rest and symptom management with medications, but emerging research supports active intervention with microcurrent neurostimulation to accelerate the brain's natural healing processes.

How Microcurrent Works

Microcurrent electrical neuromuscular stimulation delivers sub-sensory electrical currents that mirror the body's own bioelectric signals. At the cellular level, these currents stimulate mitochondrial activity, increasing adenosine triphosphate (ATP) production by up to 500%. ATP is the primary energy currency of cells and is essential for tissue repair, inflammation resolution, and neural recovery.

★

Why It Helps Concussions

Traumatic brain injury disrupts cellular metabolism in affected brain regions. Neurons that have been damaged by concussive forces experience an energy crisis—they need more ATP to repair themselves but their mitochondria are compromised. By providing external microcurrent stimulation, the Micro-Z device helps restore normal cellular energy production, supporting the brain's natural repair mechanisms.

Treatment Experience

Micro-Z treatment sessions are painless and non-invasive. The sub-sensory current is below the threshold of perception, meaning patients typically feel nothing during treatment. Sessions are prescribed based on the severity and duration of symptoms, with many patients reporting improvement in headache frequency, cognitive clarity, and energy levels within the first few weeks of treatment.

Important Information

When to Seek Concussion Treatment

Seek Immediate Medical Attention For:

  • ✓Loss of consciousness lasting more than a few seconds
  • ✓Worsening headache that does not improve
  • ✓Repeated vomiting or nausea
  • ✓Seizures or convulsions
  • ✓Slurred speech or confusion that worsens
  • ✓Weakness, numbness, or coordination problems

Consider Microcurrent Therapy If:

  • ✓Concussion symptoms persist beyond 2–4 weeks
  • ✓Headaches, brain fog, or fatigue are not improving
  • ✓Difficulty concentrating is affecting work or school
  • ✓Standard rest and medication are not providing relief
  • ✓You want a drug-free approach to recovery
  • ✓Multiple concussions have been sustained over time
