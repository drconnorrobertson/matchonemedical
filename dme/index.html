Our Product Line

Advanced Pain Management Technology

Each device in the Match One Medical product line is FDA-cleared, clinically validated, and designed for both clinical and home use.

Thermal Therapy

Therm-X

The Therm-X is a portable thermal therapy system that delivers controlled hot and cold therapy to targeted body areas. Using circulating temperature-regulated fluid through anatomically designed wraps, the Therm-X provides consistent, therapeutic-grade temperature application that far exceeds the effectiveness of ice packs or heating pads.

The device features adjustable temperature controls, programmable therapy cycles, and interchangeable wraps for the knee, shoulder, back, hip, and ankle. It is indicated for post-surgical recovery, acute injury management, chronic pain conditions, and rehabilitation protocols.

  • ✓Continuous-flow thermal regulation
  • ✓Programmable hot/cold cycles
  • ✓Multiple anatomical wrap options
  • ✓FDA-cleared for home and clinical use
Heat + Cold + Compression

VascuTherm 5

The VascuTherm 5 is the flagship multi-modality device in the Match One Medical product line. It combines three proven therapeutic modalities in a single, portable unit: controlled heat therapy, controlled cold therapy, and intermittent pneumatic compression.

This three-in-one approach addresses pain, inflammation, and edema simultaneously. The VascuTherm 5 features five programmable treatment protocols, independent temperature and compression controls, and medical-grade wraps designed for the major joint areas. It is the centerpiece of our opioid reduction program.

  • ✓Combined heating, cooling, and compression
  • ✓Five pre-set treatment protocols
  • ✓Independent temperature and pressure controls
  • ✓Medical-grade, washable anatomical wraps
Heat + Cold + Compression

VascuTherm 2

The VascuTherm 2 delivers the same proven combination of thermal therapy and pneumatic compression as the VascuTherm 5 in a more compact, cost-effective package. Microcurrent Stimulation

Micro-Z

The Micro-Z is a portable microcurrent electrical neuromuscular stimulation (MENS) device that delivers sub-sensory electrical currents to promote tissue healing at the cellular level. Unlike traditional TENS units that mask pain through nerve stimulation, the Micro-Z works at the cellular level to increase ATP production, accelerate tissue repair, and reduce inflammation.

Patients using the Micro-Z typically feel no sensation during treatment, yet clinical studies demonstrate significant improvements in healing rates and pain reduction. The device is indicated for soft tissue injuries, post-surgical healing, chronic pain syndromes, and is a key component of our concussion therapy protocol.

  • ✓Sub-sensory microcurrent technology
  • ✓Promotes ATP production and cellular repair
  • ✓Compact, battery-powered, wearable
  • ✓Used in concussion therapy protocols
