Our Approach

Multi-Modality Pain Management

Match One Medical's opioid reduction program combines multiple evidence-based, non-pharmacological treatment modalities into a single, integrated protocol. Rather than relying on any one technology, our approach layers complementary therapies to address pain at multiple physiological levels.

  • ✓Thermal Therapy: Alternating heat and cold application using our Therm-X and VascuTherm devices to reduce inflammation, increase blood flow, and block pain signal transmission at the nerve level.
  • ✓Pneumatic Compression: Intermittent compression therapy via VascuTherm devices reduces edema, accelerates healing, and provides measurable pain relief in post-surgical and acute injury patients.
  • ✓Microcurrent Stimulation: Sub-sensory electrical stimulation through the Micro-Z device promotes ATP production at the cellular level, accelerating tissue repair and reducing pain without any sensation or side effects.
  • ✓Spinal Orthotics: Custom-fitted and prefabricated bracing provides structural support that reduces mechanical pain in the spine, decreasing the need for pharmacological intervention.
Clinical Evidence

Why Drug-Free Modalities Work

Our treatment approach is grounded in decades of peer-reviewed clinical research supporting non-pharmacological pain management.

Thermal Therapy Research

Clinical studies have demonstrated that controlled thermal therapy reduces post-operative pain scores by up to 50% compared to standard protocols. Cryotherapy applied within the first 48 hours post-surgery has been shown to significantly decrease narcotic consumption while improving range of motion recovery timelines.

Compression Therapy Studies

Intermittent pneumatic compression has been validated in multiple randomized controlled trials as an effective adjunct for post-surgical recovery. Studies show reduced swelling, faster rehabilitation milestones, and decreased reliance on pain medication when compression is integrated into recovery protocols.

Microcurrent Evidence

Microcurrent electrical neuromuscular stimulation (MENS) has been studied extensively for its role in tissue repair and pain reduction. Research indicates that sub-sensory microcurrent stimulation increases ATP production by up to 500%, accelerating the body's natural healing processes without the risks associated with pharmacological pain control.

How It Works

The Match One Protocol

Step 1: Patient Assessment

The physician evaluates the patient's pain condition, surgical status, and medical history to determine the appropriate combination of drug-free modalities.

Step 2: Device Selection & Fitting

Based on the assessment, the appropriate combination of thermal therapy, compression, microcurrent, and/or bracing devices is selected and fitted to the patient.

Step 3: Treatment Protocol Initiation

The patient begins a structured treatment protocol, typically involving multiple daily sessions with their prescribed device combination, with clear documentation and follow-up milestones.

Step 4: Monitoring & Adjustment

Progress is tracked through standardized pain assessment tools. The protocol is adjusted as the patient progresses, with the goal of full pain resolution without narcotic dependency.
