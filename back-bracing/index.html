Back Bracing & Spinal Orthotics

Professional-grade spinal support solutions that reduce pain, promote healing, and help patients avoid unnecessary opioid use.

Spinal Support Solutions

Structural Support for Pain Relief

Back bracing and spinal orthotics are a cornerstone of conservative pain management. By providing external structural support to the spine, orthotic devices reduce mechanical stress on injured or degenerative tissues, decrease muscle spasm, and allow the body to heal without pharmacological intervention.

Match One Medical offers a comprehensive range of spinal orthotic solutions, from prefabricated braces for acute conditions to custom-molded devices for complex spinal pathologies. Every brace we provide is selected and fitted to meet the specific clinical needs of the patient, ensuring optimal support and comfort.

Product Range

Types of Braces We Offer

Comprehensive spinal orthotic solutions for every level of the spine and a wide range of clinical indications.

⚗

Lumbar Support Braces (LSO)

Lumbosacral orthoses provide targeted support to the lower back. Ideal for lumbar disc herniation, degenerative disc disease, spinal stenosis, post-surgical stabilization after lumbar procedures, and chronic lower back pain. Available in rigid, semi-rigid, and flexible configurations.

⚗

Thoracolumbar Braces (TLSO)

Thoracolumbosacral orthoses extend support from the thoracic spine through the lumbar region. Prescribed for compression fractures, post-spinal fusion stabilization, scoliosis management, and thoracolumbar injuries. Custom-molded options available for complex cases.

⚗

Cervical Orthoses

Cervical collars and cervicothoracic braces for neck injuries, cervical disc conditions, post-surgical immobilization, and whiplash-associated disorders. Ranging from soft foam collars for mild support to rigid devices for significant cervical instability.

⚗

Sacroiliac (SI) Belts

Targeted compression belts for sacroiliac joint dysfunction, a common and often underdiagnosed source of lower back and pelvic pain. These devices stabilize the SI joint during activity, reducing pain and improving functional capacity.

Insurance Coverage & Fitting Process

Insurance & Medicare Coverage

Most spinal orthotic devices are covered under Medicare, Medicaid, and major private insurance plans when prescribed by a physician for a documented medical condition. Match One Medical handles all insurance verification, prior authorization, coding, and billing on behalf of the prescribing physician's office.

We work with all major insurance carriers and are experienced in navigating the documentation requirements for orthotic device approval, ensuring that patients receive the devices they need without out-of-pocket surprise costs.
