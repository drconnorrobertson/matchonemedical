Why Partner With Us

Better Outcomes. Lower Risk. New Revenue.

Physicians today face mounting pressure to reduce opioid prescribing while still providing effective pain management. Match One Medical gives your practice the tools, training, and billing infrastructure to offer FDA-cleared, drug-free pain management as a standard part of your care delivery.

Our turnkey partnership program is designed to integrate seamlessly into your existing workflows. We handle the complexity of DME procurement, insurance verification, coding, billing, and compliance so your team can focus on patient care.

Reduced Opioid Liability

Every opioid prescription carries clinical, legal, and regulatory risk. Patients treated with our multi-modality protocols report higher satisfaction scores, faster return to function, and fewer complications compared to opioid-managed patients.
