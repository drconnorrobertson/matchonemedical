Our Mission

Fighting the Opioid Crisis With Innovation

Founded by Chaz Jannuzi, Match One Medical was built on a simple belief: no patient should have to choose between living with pain and risking addiction. By combining cutting-edge thermal therapy, pneumatic compression, and microcurrent technology, we give physicians a complete drug-free pain management toolkit.

Our programs have helped thousands of patients reduce or eliminate their reliance on opioid medications while experiencing meaningful, measurable pain relief.
