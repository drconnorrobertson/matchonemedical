Insurance & Financial Assistance

We believe cost should never be a barrier to drug-free pain management. Learn about your coverage options and our financial assistance programs.

Insurance Coverage

We Work With Your Insurance

Match One Medical's durable medical equipment and spinal orthotic products are covered by most major insurance plans when prescribed by a physician for a documented medical condition. Our dedicated insurance team handles the entire verification and authorization process on your behalf, so you can focus on your recovery.

We accept Medicare, Medicaid, and most private insurance carriers. Our billing team has extensive experience navigating the documentation and coding requirements specific to DME and orthotic devices, ensuring the smoothest possible claims experience for both patients and referring physicians.

Coverage Details

What Is Typically Covered

Medicare Coverage

Medicare Part B covers durable medical equipment when it is prescribed by a physician and meets medical necessity criteria. Our thermal therapy devices, compression systems, microcurrent stimulators, and spinal orthotics all fall under recognized HCPCS billing codes. Match One Medical is an enrolled Medicare DME supplier, and we handle all claims submission and follow-up.

Private Insurance

Most private insurance plans cover DME and orthotic devices when medically necessary. Coverage levels, copays, and deductible requirements vary by plan. Our insurance verification team contacts your carrier directly to confirm your specific coverage, estimate your out-of-pocket costs, and obtain any required prior authorizations before your device is ordered.

Workers' Compensation

Match One Medical devices are frequently prescribed for workplace injuries covered under workers' compensation programs. We have experience working with workers' comp carriers and understand the specific documentation and approval requirements for these claims.

Payment Options

Flexible Payment Plans

For patients with insurance copays, deductibles, or for those without insurance coverage, Match One Medical offers flexible payment plans to make our devices accessible. We understand that medical expenses can be a significant burden, and we work with each patient individually to find a payment arrangement that fits their budget.

Financial Hardship

Financial Hardship Programs

Match One Medical is committed to ensuring that no patient is denied access to drug-free pain management due to financial constraints.

Hardship Application

Patients who are experiencing financial hardship may apply for reduced-cost or no-cost access to our devices and services. Our financial counselors review each application individually, taking into account household income, insurance status, medical expenses, and other relevant factors. We are committed to finding a path to treatment for every patient who needs it.

How to Apply

To apply for financial assistance, contact our patient services team by phone or through our contact form. A financial counselor will guide you through the application process, which typically involves providing basic income documentation and a brief explanation of your financial situation. Decisions are made quickly so that treatment is not delayed.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a prescription to get a device? +

Yes. All Match One Medical devices require a physician prescription. This is both a clinical requirement and an insurance requirement. If you believe one of our devices could help with your condition, speak with your doctor about a referral.

How long does insurance verification take? +

Most insurance verifications are completed within 2 to 5 business days. In cases where prior authorization is required, the process may take slightly longer. Our team keeps you informed throughout the process and works to resolve any issues quickly.

What if my insurance denies coverage? +

If your insurance carrier denies coverage, our billing team will review the denial and, when appropriate, file an appeal on your behalf. We also work with you to explore alternative coverage options, payment plans, or financial assistance programs to ensure you can still receive treatment.

Will I have out-of-pocket costs? +

Out-of-pocket costs depend on your specific insurance plan, including your deductible status, copay requirements, and coinsurance percentages. We provide an estimate of your expected out-of-pocket costs before your device is ordered, so there are no surprises.

Do you accept Medicare Advantage plans? +

Yes, we work with most Medicare Advantage plans. Coverage specifics vary by plan, so our team will verify your particular benefits before proceeding with any device order.
